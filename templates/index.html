🧬 Real example — TP53 R175H (most common cancer mutation)

Ref: ATGGATTTCCAGTTCAGGGCCAGCTACAGT... (TP53 wild-type CDS) Mut: Same sequence with G→C at position 523 (codon 175: CGT→CAT) Protein: TP53

TP53 is mutated in >50% of all human cancers. R175H is the most frequent hotspot mutation — it disrupts the zinc-binding domain, collapses the DNA-binding loop, and completely abolishes tumour suppression. PDB: 2OCJ · UniProt: P04637

Accepts plain sequence or FASTA format. Only A, T, G, C characters are used.
